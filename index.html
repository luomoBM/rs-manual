Ragdoll Soccer Manual

A compact reference for Ragdoll Soccer, the free rope-physics football game by RHM Interactive (Unity WebGL). Checked against the live site on 2026-11-17.

The core mechanic

Every footballer is a floppy ragdoll hanging from a rope: pull the rope back, swing, and release at the right moment. Maximum swing speed sits at the bottom of the arc, so the release point near it carries the momentum into the ball. Direction comes from where you let go, not from a direction key - the home page guide builds every tip on this variable.

The two modes

Solo is ten puzzle levels - ten small geometry problems where release timing gets built with free resets (about page counts them). Two-player splits one keyboard down the middle: Player 1 takes the left half on W, A, S, D and space; Player 2 takes the right half on the arrow keys and L. The 2 player guide documents every key from a real match, photographed the day it was played.

School play, honestly framed

Playing unblocked takes one click: press Kick off, wait out the short ad that funds the free version, play. There are no mirrors - the game lives on one official domain, and the unblocked page states that plainly along with the realistic options when a network blocks the domain.

The business model

One short advertisement funds everything: no accounts, no premium tiers, per the about page, which also invites players to brag about goals or complain about lost limbs in the comments. The game itself loads in one click from the home page.
